Design Study · Pendant Lighting

The Blue Fish Chandelier

Design, 3D modelling and technical documentation of a four-tier pendant luminaire

Video 1. Rendered animation of the final design.

Abstract

This study documents the development of “The Blue Fish”, a suspended luminaire made of four undulating tiers of brass-finish arms that carry translucent blue slices patterned after banded agate, lit by warm exposed bulbs. It follows the project from material concept to 3D model in Rhino, then to the AutoCAD construction drawings and to a visual simulation of the piece in an interior.

Keywords: pendant lighting, interior design, 3D modelling, technical drawings, banded agate
1Concept and material

Concept and Material Language

The overall form draws on flowing water and the body of a fish. Wavy arms shorten tier by tier, giving the mass a balanced conical silhouette. Each blue slice carries concentric colour bands, echoing the natural growth rings of agate, and its translucent core glows when lit from within.

23D modelling

Three-Dimensional Modelling

The luminaire was modelled in Rhino. Arms, slices and bulbs were built as separate elements and assembled on the central stem. The recording below shows the surface mesh and the model reviewed from several angles before the drawings were produced.

4Visualisation

Visual Simulation in Context

To test the piece in use, it was placed in a dining room. As a focal element it draws the eye upward, and its warm light passes through the translucent slices to tint the surroundings blue.

Project Summary
